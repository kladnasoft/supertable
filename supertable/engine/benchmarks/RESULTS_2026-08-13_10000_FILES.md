# IslandDB 10,000-file AUTO and local-scan optimization

## Workload

- 10,000 independent local ZSTD Parquet files and row groups.
- 10,000 rows with globally unique row IDs.
- 19,929,744 physical source bytes; 780,000 projected compressed bytes.
- `SELECT COUNT(metric), SUM(metric) FROM events` scans every file.
- Exact IslandDB result digest matched the DuckDB oracle before timing.
- Fresh worker process for each engine; one cold and three warm executions.

## Result

| Metric | DuckDB | IslandDB before | IslandDB after |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cold wall | 23.46 s | 25.69 s | **14.62 s** |
| Warm median | 20.13 s | 23.50 s | **14.60 s** |
| Cold RSS delta | 346.6 MiB | 279.6 MiB | **42.8 MiB** |

IslandDB changed from 16.7% slower to 27.5% faster than DuckDB's warm median.
The optimization removes local-only cryptographic footer hashing and duplicate
Arrow fragment construction. Local execution already uses Polars' conservative
native Parquet pruning; remote range execution retains exact sealed fragments.

## AUTO policy

The org-scoped Redis engine document now accepts non-overlapping half-open
`auto_policy` intervals. Rules select an engine using the complete effective
scan estimate, but remain subordinate to capability, resource, Spark fleet,
tombstone, and semantic safety gates. Invalid/unavailable matches fall through
to the adaptive cost model.
